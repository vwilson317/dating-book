Chapter 00 • Full Excerpt Mostly Harmless

No one said dating would be easy. That couldn’t be more true when you’re dating abroad and don’t speak the language.

This book is first and foremost an account of my personal lived experiences dating and traveling as a now 38-year-old man from the USA. I’ve lived in Rio for almost three years and I don’t speak Portuguese, and before that I spent several years as a digital nomad.

“My vocabulary is still ‘obrigado,’ ‘cerveja,’ and a smile that says, ‘Hello, I’m mostly harmless.’”

The first story that comes to mind is my first “date” in Medellín. It ended with her berating me in English, in public, for an hour... Looking back, I should have climbed out the bathroom window instead.
