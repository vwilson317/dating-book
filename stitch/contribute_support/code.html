Clarity & Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

I wrote this book as an honest, unfiltered dump of lived misadventures, not to build a gated subscription community. I believe funny stories and hard-learned travel lessons should be accessible with zero friction: no email signup required, no credit card forms, and no paywalled chapters.
Every dollar goes straight into running ads (Instagram, Reddit travel forums) and distribution initiatives so other travelers, digital nomads, and readers discover the book before making their own catastrophic dating decisions in South America.
New chapters always launch on Instagram first (@vincent_jevon) before going live on the web reader and audio feeds. Contributors to the story tier get early alerts in their DMs.
Audio narrations are recorded for each chapter directly on their respective chapter reader pages. A physical print edition is currently being compiled; Tier 03 backers will receive priority acknowledgments and digital commemorative proofs.
